SRNE Solar Monitor

Designed for the SRNE HESP4860S100-H hybrid inverter. Uses the SolarmanV5 protocol (port 8899) via the LSW-5 WiFi data logger dongle.

Connection Requirement

The LSW-5 dongle must be plugged into the RS485 port of the HESP4860S100-H. The dongle creates a WiFi hotspot — connect your phone to it, then open its web UI at http://192.168.10.100 (user: admin, pass: admin) to find the Logger Serial Number needed below.
