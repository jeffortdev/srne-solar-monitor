Connection Requirement

An external SRNE WiFi RS485 dongle must be connected to the RS485/Modbus port of the HESP4860S100-H. The dongle broadcasts a WiFi hotspot (SSID: AP_XXXXXX) — connect your phone to it before adding a device.
